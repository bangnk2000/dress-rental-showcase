## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the romantic, poetic atmosphere of a high-end Parisian atelier infused with the gentle, welcoming charm of Hanoi’s modern boutique scene. Targeted at fashion-conscious women seeking elevated, dreamlike wardrobes for celebrations, travels, and artistic photo shoots, the experience prioritizes grace, visual serenity, and tactile elegance. 

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Parisian Chic**:
- **Atmosphere:** Sun-drenched linen, porcelain cream tones, dried rose petals, and delicate gold accents.
- **Visual Stance:** Uncluttered, expansive negative space that frames fashion photography like gallery art. 
- **Emotional Resonance:** Intimate, luxurious, empowering, and effortlessly poetic.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from vintage blush silks, warm ivory parchment, and antique gilded mirrors, avoiding harsh blacks or synthetic tones in favor of grounded, velvety shades.

- **Primary (`#8A4F58` - Deep Vintage Rose):** Anchors actions, emphasized headings, and key focus states. A rich, sophisticated muted berry-rose that delivers contrast without sterile severity.
- **Secondary (`#D4A3A1` - Dusty Blush Silk):** Used for tender accents, soft tag highlights, category cards, and subtle active indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A880` - Warm Champagne Gold):** Reserved for rental availability highlights, VIP status badges, star ratings, and delicate embellishments.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FBF8F5` - Warm Ivory Cream):** The foundational canvas. Replaces clinical white with a warm, luminous surface.
- **Deep Text Neutral (`#2E2625` - Deep Espresso Brown):** Replaces pure black for typography and high-contrast lines, maintaining an organic, editorial softness.
- **Subtle Border Neutral (`#EADBDA` - Spun Bisque):** Whisper-soft lines that delineate items without dividing space abruptly.

## Typography

Typography balances evocative poetic expression with flawless mobile commerce legibility.

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** High-contrast, romantic, and reminiscent of high-fashion print magazines. It conveys pedigree and feminine poise. Italics should be peppered deliberately on key evocative words (e.g., *Muse*, *Atelier*, *Collection*).
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Its rounded geometry and clear stroke modulation preserve warmth while guaranteeing smooth legibility on small screens across Vietnamese diacritics and inventory specifications.
- **Case Styling:** Eyebrow labels, dates, dress size tags, and metadata take an uppercase styling with expansive letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mimic luxury garment tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adopts a deliberate, breathing rhythm structured on a generous 12-column grid for desktop/tablet and a 4-column flow for mobile.

- **Editorial Pacing:** Ample outer margins (`margin: 3rem`) isolate each dress as a curated exhibition piece rather than crowded commercial stock.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Hero banners and seasonal lookbooks alternate between centered editorial stacks and left-weighted asymmetric imagery paired with overlapping floating narrative cards.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections are separated by generous spacing blocks (`space-xl`), punctuated occasionally by gossamer-thin ornamental separator rules or small gilded floral motifs.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids harsh dropshadows or heavy Material-style z-planes. Depth is whisper-quiet and airy:

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#FBF8F5`. Floating cards, detail drawers, and filter trays sit on `#FFFFFF` with a sheer, warm rose-tinted border (`#EADBDA`).
- **Luminescent Ambient Shadows:** When elevation is required (such as sticky booking bars or modal sheets), use an extended soft diffusion: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(138, 79, 88, 0.07)`. The tint uses the primary rose rather than neutral gray to sustain warmth.
- **Frosted Silk Glassmorphism:** Sticky navigation bars and floating photo carousel controls leverage `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with an 85% translucent ivory fill (`rgba(251, 248, 245, 0.85)`).

## Shapes

Curves mirror soft silk drapes and vintage vanity mirrors:

- **Corner Radii:** Cards, inputs, and containment modules maintain a relaxed `rounded-md` (8px / 0.5rem) to `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) corner curve.
- **Interactive Pills:** Primary call-to-actions, category filter pills, and sizing badges take full pill profiles (`rounded-full`) to offer an approachable, touch-friendly sensation.
- **Arched Frames:** Occasional hero collection previews utilize Romanesque rounded-arch tops (e.g., `rounded-t-[80px]`) to evoke classic Parisian boutique doorways and ornate floor mirrors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Thuê Ngay" / Rent Now):** Solid deep vintage rose background (`#8A4F58`) with crisp ivory text, pill curvature, and subtle hover transition shifting to `#733F47`.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Xem Chi Tiết" / View Lookbook):** Warm ivory base with a delicate 1px border (`#D4A3A1`), primary-colored text, and subtle blush glow on hover.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Plain text in uppercase `label-lg`, underlined with a slender 1px offset accent line in `#C5A880`.

### Product & Dress Rental Cards
- **Image Treatment:** Aspect ratio of 3:4 or 4:5 with soft `rounded-lg` contours. Soft zoom-on-hover effect.
- **Tag Overlay:** Floating translucent pill in the top-left indicating availability ("Có sẵn", "Đang được thuê", "Mới về").
- **Pricing Display:** Rental price highlighted in primary vintage rose paired with the original retail value crossed out in subtle warm neutral text.

### Date Picker & Rental Calendar
- Custom dual-date selector for rental windows (3-day or 5-day slots).
- Selected range represented by a continuous soft blush ribbon (`#F3E3E2`) anchored by rounded primary rose endpoints.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Clean ivory background with inset padding, `rounded-md` radius, and 1px border in `#EADBDA`.
- On focus: border transitions to `#8A4F58` with a gentle 3px outer aura of `rgba(212, 163, 161, 0.25)`.
- Floating labels set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` tracking `label-sm`.

### Chips & Dress Filter Selectors
- Pill-shaped tags for dress lengths (Mini, Midi, Maxi) and occasions (Tiệc, Cưới, Nàng Thơ, Du Lịch).
- Inactive: Background `#F5EFEA`, text muted brown.
- Active: Background `#8A4F58`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Custom rounded rings in `#D4A3A1`.
- Checked state fills with `#8A4F58` featuring a micro gold star or centered dot, eschewing standard utilitarian ticks.
